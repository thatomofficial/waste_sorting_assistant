## Data Retention

### Retention Periods
We retain your information for different periods based on type and purpose:

**Account Data:**
- **Active accounts:** Until account deletion
- **Inactive accounts:** 3 years of inactivity
- **Deleted accounts:** 30 days (then permanently deleted)

**Usage Data:**
- **Analytics data:** 26 months
- **Crash reports:** 90 days
- **Support communications:** 3 years

**Image Data:**
- **Scan images:** Processed immediately, then deleted
- **Processing results:** Retained for personalization
- **ML training data:** Anonymized and retained indefinitely

### Automatic Deletion
We automatically delete:
- Temporary files and caches
- Expired session tokens
- Old backup files
- Inactive user data per schedule

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requirements:
